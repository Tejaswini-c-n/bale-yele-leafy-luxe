# Bale Yele Digital

Build a premium, visually immersive, modern website for **BALE YELE – Taste of North Karnataka**, an authentic vegetarian North Karnataka restaurant in Rajarajeshwari Nagar (RR Nagar), Bengaluru.

## 1. BRAND & PURPOSE

Bale Yele is a vegetarian restaurant focused on authentic North Karnataka cuisine, traditional flavours, homely food and the experience of eating meals served fresh on banana leaves.

The restaurant currently does not have a dedicated website.

The primary purpose of this website is to:

* Establish a strong online identity for Bale Yele
* Make the restaurant look premium, authentic and trustworthy
* Showcase its food and North Karnataka heritage
* Help customers discover the restaurant through Google
* Encourage customers to visit the restaurant
* Make it extremely easy to call, get directions and view the menu
* Create a memorable visual experience that differentiates Bale Yele from ordinary local restaurant websites

Do NOT make this look like a generic food-delivery website or a basic restaurant template.

The website should feel like **North Karnataka culture translated into a contemporary digital experience.**
